Paris

Paris, the capital of France, is known for its beautiful architecture, rich history, romantic atmosphere, and world-famous landmarks. It is often called the "City of Light" and attracts millions of visitors every year.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Eiffel Tower – the iconic symbol of Paris
  2. Louvre Museum – home of the Mona Lisa
  3. Notre-Dame Cathedral – historic Gothic architecture
  4. Champs-Élysées – famous shopping avenue
  5. Montmartre – artistic hilltop district

Must-Try Foods

  • Croissants – buttery and flaky pastry
  • Macarons – colorful sweet treats
  • French Cheese – rich and flavorful varieties
  • Crêpes – thin pancakes with sweet or savory fillings
  • Baguette – classic French bread
